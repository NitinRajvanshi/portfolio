About

Building software that ships, not just demos.

I'm a pre-final year Electronics and Computer Engineering undergraduate at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology, Patiala. My work sits at the intersection of full-stack development, cloud infrastructure, and AI/ML systems — turning ambiguous requirements into working, deployable software.

That's meant building a full-stack web vulnerability scanner with an OWASP-based scanning architecture; engineering a federated learning intrusion-detection system that trains across simulated clients without exposing raw data, secured with Paillier homomorphic encryption; and interning at TIET's Experiential Learning Centre on LiverGuard, an AI-assisted IoT liver-health monitoring system built on ESP32 sensors, Flask, and AWS.

Beyond the codebase, I lead the Energy Conservation Club as General Secretary — running a 40+ member team through sprint-based planning and stand-ups, and I'm drawn to cybersecurity and IoT security as a throughline across my project work.
